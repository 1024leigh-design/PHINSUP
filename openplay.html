Onside and offside in open play

  • In general play you're offside if you're in front of a teammate who has the ball (or just kicked it) and you get involved.
  • Once a ruck forms, the offside line is the hindmost foot. If you're not in the ruck, get behind it.

Defense basics

  • Line speed: move up together, fast.
  • Folding: run around behind the ruck to get back in the line.
