Kickoffs & Restarts

  • WhereFrom the center of halfway.
  • WhenAt the start of each half and after scores.
  • Who kicksAfter a score, the team that got scored on kicks off to the team that scored. At the start of the game a coin toss decides, and the other team kicks off the second half.
  • RuleThe kick must go 10 meters (reach the 10m line).
  • FrontFront row (1, 2, 3) up front, by the 10m line.
  • Behind themLocks (4, 5) back, then the 8.
  • WideFlankers (6, 7) watch the sidelines.
  • MiddleThe 9 in the middle, with the 15 behind them.

Receiving a kickoff: think of it as an exploded scrum.

Drop outs

22 drop out

Restart with a drop kick from the 22 when the attacking team kicks or carries the ball dead in your in-goal.

Goal line drop out

Restart from your own goal line in certain situations, like when the ball is held up in your in-goal.

If the kickoff goes wrong

  • Doesn't reach 10m: the other team chooses a re-kick or a scrum at halfway.
  • Goes straight out (on the full): the other team chooses a re-kick, a scrum at halfway, or a lineout at halfway.

Catching safely under pressure

  • Call for it loud and early.
  • Eyes on the ball, hands up, bring it into your chest.
  • Teammates get in close to support the catcher.

Chasing kicks

  • Chase as a line, not one at a time.
  • You can only play the ball if you were behind the kicker when they kicked.
