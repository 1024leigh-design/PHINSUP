    How advantage works

    After a foul, the ref can let play go on if the other team benefits. If they don't gain anything, the ref goes back and gives the penalty (or scrum).

    Yellow vs red cards

    • Yellow: 10 minutes in the sin bin. Your team plays a player down.
    • Red: sent off for the rest of the match.
    • Giving away the same penalty again and again (repeat infringement) can earn a yellow card.

    The 10 meter law

    At a penalty, the team that gave it away must get back 10 meters right away. If they don't, the penalty moves 10 meters forward.

    How refs manage the breakdown

    Refs talk a lot at the ruck: "Release!", "Roll away!", "Hands off!", "Use it!" Do what they say right away and you'll avoid most penalties.

    Laws can differ

    Competitions can play slightly different laws, and World Rugby law trials change things over time. Check with your coach about what applies to us.
