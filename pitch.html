Kicking into touch: inside vs outside your 22

  • From inside your own 22: if the kick goes straight out (on the full), the lineout is where it went out. You gain ground.
  • From outside your 22: if it goes out on the full, the lineout is back level with where you kicked it. No ground gained, so bounce it in first.
  • Penalty kicks to touch are different: the lineout is where it goes out, and the kicking team throws in.

The 50:22

Kick from inside your own half, the ball bounces in the field and then goes out inside their 22: your team gets the throw. (The ball can't be passed or carried back into your half first.)
