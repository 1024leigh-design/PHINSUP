Our Shape: 1-3-3-1

1-3-3-1 means how our 8 forwards spread across the field in open play.

  • 1A flanker on one edge (sideline to the 15m line).
  • 3A center pod (tight five + 8).
  • 3A center pod (tight five + 8).
  • 1A flanker on the other edge (sideline to the 15m line).

The tight five are in a center pod. You carry the ball in a pod of 3.
