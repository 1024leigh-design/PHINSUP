The Lineout

  • WhenThe ball goes out of bounds (into touch).
  • ThrowOur hooker always throws, straight down the middle.
  • SetupFront lifter, two lifter/jumpers, back lifter. Everyone else is in the back pod.
  • BallThe jumper catches or taps it down to the 9, or we form a maul.

Who's where

Learn More 🤓
  • The throw must be straight down the middle.
  • Safe lifting: lift on the thighs/shorts, hold, and bring the jumper down safely. Never drop the jumper.
  • Front, middle, and back throws: the call tells the hooker which jumper to aim for.
  • Dummy jumps fool the other team about where the throw is going.
  • Lineout to maul: the jumper lands with the ball, teammates bind on, and the group drives forward. Mauls →
  • On the Phins, any forward can lift, jump, or be in the back pod. The usual "locks jump, props lift" rule isn't us. Flexibility over labels.
