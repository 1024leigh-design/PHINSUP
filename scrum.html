The Scrum

  • WhoAll 8 forwards from each team.
  • WhenTo restart after small mistakes, like a knock-on or forward pass.
  • Shape: 3-2-33 in the front row (props + hooker), 2 in the second row (locks), 3 in the back row (flankers + 8).
  • Body positionEveryone: hips just above knees, back flat, chin up.
  • The callsThe ref calls Crouch, Bind, Set.

Loosehead vs tighthead

The loosehead (1) has one opponent's head next to theirs. The tighthead (3) has two, so they take pressure from both sides. See the Prop card →

Hooking vs channeling to the 8

The hooker hooks the ball back with their foot. It then travels back through the scrum to the base, where the 8 controls it until it's time to play.

When can each player break off?

  • Flankers stay bound until the ball is out.
  • The 8 can pick the ball up from the base (the eight man pick).

Brake foot

We use the brake foot rule. Hookers, ask your coach to show you the brake foot position.
