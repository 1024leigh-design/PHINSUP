Safe Tackling Basics 💪

🍑Cheek to cheek

Your head goes on the side of their butt/hip, never in front of their body.

👀Eyes open, chin up

See what you're hitting.

🤗Wrap with your arms

Arms around, hold on.

🦵Drive with your legs

Short, powerful steps through the tackle.

🎯Target: below the chest

Aim low. Never above the shoulders.
