How subs work

Subs come on at a stoppage, and the ref has to know about it. Wait on the sideline until you're waved on.

What to expect from the ref

  • The ref talks a lot: "Release!", "Roll away!", "Use it!" Just do it right away.
  • Only the captain talks to the ref about decisions.
  • Learn the ref signalsso you know what's happening.
