Match day timeline

  1. Arrival. Our match secretary posts the time. It's usually about 2 hours before kickoff.
  2. Warm up. Starts about 1 hour before kickoff, together as a team.
  3. Team talk. Listen up, ask questions.
  4. Kickoff!
  5. Halftime. Water, quick talk, switch ends.
  6. Game ends. We win! 🏆
  7. Post-match social. Both teams hang out after. This is not optional: showing up is part of being a Phin.
Be careful! Parking and walking to the field takes longer than you think. It's way better to be there early and yap with friends than to be late and panicking.

How subs work

Subs come on at a stoppage, and the ref has to know about it. Wait on the sideline until you're waved on.

What to expect from the ref

NO ONE talks to the ref except the captains. Not about calls, not about anything. If you have a question, tell a captain.
  • ListenThe ref talks a lot: "Release!", "Roll away!", "Use it!" Just do it right away.
  • Captains onlyOnly captains talk to the ref.
  • SignalsLearn the ref signals so you know what's happening.
It's ok to be nervous. Everyone was new once. Never go alone: your team has your back.
