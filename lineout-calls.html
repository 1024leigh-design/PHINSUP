Forward Calls: Lineouts

Every call: a front lifter, a back lifter, and two lifter/jumpers in the middle. Everyone else is in the back pod. J = jumper, L = lifter, H = hooker. Their line is the dark row.

Flexibility over labels. Any forward can lift, jump, or be in the back pod. The only set job is the hooker's throw.
