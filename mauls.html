Phins Playbook

Mauls

Ball carrier on their feet, held by an opponent, with at least one teammate bound on.

The basics

  1. Form. The ball carrier stays on their feet. Teammates bind on right away. Never go alone.
  2. Bind. Bind with your whole arm, stay low, stay on your feet.
  3. Drive. Short, powerful steps, all together. One heart, one beat.
  4. Ball to the back. Pass it back hand to hand so the player at the back controls it.
  5. Keep it moving. If the maul stops, the ref will say "Use it!" and the ball has to come out quickly.
