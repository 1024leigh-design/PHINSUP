Concussion & Safety 🧠

🛑
If in doubt, sit them out. A player with a suspected concussion does not go back on that day.

Signs of concussion

Any one of these is enough to stop

What you might see

Lying still or slow to get up, unsteady on their feet, dazed or blank look, confused, holding their head.

What they might feel

Headache, dizziness, feeling sick, blurry vision, sensitive to light or noise, "not right," trouble remembering.

🚨 Call 911

Neck pain, knocked out, a seizure, repeated vomiting, a headache that gets worse, weakness or tingling in arms or legs, or getting more confused.

Who to tell

  • Tell a coach, captain, or the ref right away. About yourself or a teammate.
  • Speaking up is being a good teammate. Never let someone go alone.
