Concussion & Safety

We value your long-term health. If you feel even a little weird, come off. You don't have to be sure. You don't have to worry about the rest of the team. We want you safe and healthy.

See the trainer

  • Dizzy?See the trainer.
  • Headache?See the trainer.
  • Took a hard hit?See the trainer.

If in doubt, sit out. That goes for teammates too: if someone seems off, tell the trainer or a coach.

Preventing concussions

#1

Proper form

Tackling and running with proper form is the number one way to prevent concussions. Play by the rules and play with good form, and you keep yourself and everyone else much safer. Safe tackling basics →

Neck training

Do the neck drills we do at practice on your own time too. A strong neck helps protect your head.

Mouthguard

Always wear it. It protects your teeth and jaw, it may help lower concussion risk, and it's required to play.
