Scoring & Kicking

5 points

Try

Ground the ball in the in-goal.

2 points

Conversion

A kick at the posts after a try, taken in line with where the try was scored.

3 points

Penalty goal Backs' job

A kick at the posts from a penalty.

3 points

Drop goal Backs' job

Did you know you can kick for 3 points, kind of like a field goal in football? Drop kick it through the posts in open play. Harder and less common, but it's an option!

Learn More 🤓

Penalty try: 7 points

Awarded when foul play stops a probable try. No kick needed.

Do I need to keep score?

Nope! You don't need to keep track of the score. Coaches and captains decide when we do things like kick a penalty for points or go for a drop goal.

Why scoring under the posts matters

The conversion is taken in line with where you grounded the ball. Ground it near the posts and the kick is easy. Ground it in the corner and the kick is from a tough angle.

Kicking tee basics

  • Tees are for kicks at goal (conversions and penalty goals).
  • Set the ball on the tee, line up with the posts, and take your time.
